# Introduction
(Article of Faith 10 - “We believe in the literal gathering of Israel and in the restoration of the Ten Tribes; that Zion (the New Jerusalem) will be built upon the American continent; that Christ will reign personally upon the earth; and, that the earth will be renewed and receive its paradisiacal glory.”)
Speaking today about the gathering of Israel

# What is it
Abrahamic covenant

Abraham made covenants with God when he received the gospel, when he was ordained a high priest, and when he entered into celestial marriage. In these covenants, God promised great blessings to Abraham and his family. These blessings, which extend to all of Abraham’s seed, are called the Abrahamic covenant.
Among the promises made to Abraham were the following:
His posterity would be numerous.
His seed, or descendants, would receive the gospel and bear the priesthood.
Through the ministry of his seed, “all the families of the earth [would] be blessed, even with the blessings of the Gospel, which are the blessings of salvation, even of life eternal.”
A person can receive all the blessings of the Abrahamic covenant—even if he or she is not a literal descendant of Abraham—by obeying the laws and ordinances of the gospel.

What this teaches us is that the gathering of Israel involves blessing all of the families of the earth with the blessings of the gospel. We have each felt the blessings of God in our lives, and so we need to share those blessings with everyone around us. 

In General Conference this last April, the Prophet Russel M Nelson talked about the love God has for us. He said, “I invite you to consider carefully the following three statements:
The gathering of Israel is evidence that God loves all of His children everywhere.
The gospel of Abraham is further evidence that God loves all of His children everywhere. He invites all to come unto Him—“black and white, bond and free, male and female; … all are alike unto God.”
The sealing power is supernal evidence of how much God loves all of His children everywhere and wants each of them to choose to return home to Him.”
We can see the love of God in the gathering of Israel, meaning that His love for us is apparent in how concerned he is with making sure that we are all able to receive his blessings, engage with all of our brothers and sisters, and be sealed to live together as families forever. How wonderful a blessing! 

President Russell M. Nelson declared, “Anytime you do anything that helps anyone—on either side of the veil—take a step toward making covenants with God and receiving their essential baptismal and temple ordinances, you are helping to gather Israel. It is as simple as that” 

# How
	One was that we can gather Israel is by using the Book of Mormon
	“The coming forth of the Book of Mormon is a sign to the entire world that the Lord has commenced to gather Israel and fulfill covenants He made to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. We not only teach this doctrine, but we participate in it. We do so as we help to gather the elect of the Lord on both sides of the veil.
“The Book of Mormon is central to this work. It declares the doctrine of the gathering. It causes people to learn about Jesus Christ, to believe His gospel, and to join His Church. In fact, if there were no Book of Mormon, the promised gathering of Israel would not occur” (“The Gathering of Scattered Israel,” Ensign or Liahona, Nov. 2006, 80).” – President Nelson, 2006, The Gathering of Scattered Israel
The Book of Mormon is filled with evidence of God’s love and promises to his people, and is a powerful tool we can use when inviting others. Many people are happy to engage with religious conversations, and it is easy to simply share a story, promise, or doctrine that you have learned from the Book of Mormon. That is the gathering of Israel. 

# Additionally, missionary work is central to gathering. 
	Missionary Purpose - “Preach My Gospel teaches that a missionary’s purpose is to “invite others to come unto Christ by helping them receive the restored gospel through faith in Jesus Christ and His Atonement, repentance, baptism, receiving the gift of the Holy Ghost, and enduring to the end” (Preach My Gospel, 1).”

Missionary Work and Expecting Miracles
	Several weeks ago, when Sister Chadwick spoke, she said, “I learned very quickly that if I only claimed the brother and sisters that were “doing the right thing”, I would have a very small family”. 
	I testify that what she said is true. It is not up to us to decide who is “worth” gathering. 
When and where is the right time to gather Israel?
	The prophet Mormon in war – “And it came to pass in that same year there began to be a war again between the Nephites and the Lamanites. And notwithstanding I being young, was large in stature; therefore the people of Nephi appointed me that I should be their leader, or the leader of their armies…”
“And behold, I had employed my people, the Nephites, in preparing their lands and their arms against the time of battle. And it came to pass that the Lord did say unto me: Cry unto this people—Repent ye, and come unto me, and be ye baptized, and build up again my church, and ye shall be spared.” 
Where did Christ teach? He taught in the road, at dinner parties, in people’s places of work, at funerals, in the temple, in short, he taught wherever he was, because he knew the importance of the message and that everyone needs to hear it. 

Joseph Smith reaffirmed the necessity of inviting everyone everywhere when he said, “The standard of truth has been erected. No unhallowed hand can stop the work from progressing; persecutions may rage, mobs may combine, armies may assemble, calumny may defame, but the truth of God will go forth boldly, nobly, and independent till it has penetrated every continent, visited every clime, swept every country, and sounded in every ear, till the purposes of God shall be accomplished and the great Jehovah shall say the work is done.”

End with a story from my mission
One time on my mission, my mission president came to my companion and I on a Tuesday evening and said, “Elders, I want to challenge you to baptize someone this Saturday.” That was not common in our mission, and while we had both had success in other areas, we had yet to find anyone who was ready to be baptized in that area together. To be honest, I probably wondered about the naivety of my MP, who thought that missionary work was so easy. Well, as we discussed how we could accomplish this goal to gather Israel that week, we remembered that there was a young woman, Jessica, who came every week and participated in young women’s, who was not yet baptized because her mother would not let her. We immediately went to their house, spoke with her and her mother that now was the time to commit to following God. She was baptized that Saturday, and a call from an inspired leader had been fulfilled. As you can imagine, my mission president was happy with the success we had and a few weeks later made the same challenge to us – this time on a Thursday night. I would like to say that I had instant faith that this was possible, but it still seemed like a long shot. A woman named Vera had come to the ward for several weeks, but had already turned down several invitations to be baptized, but she was the only one we knew who was ready to be baptized that weekend. We immediately called her and invited her to meet with us, and extended an invitation to be baptized, which she accepted (as long as the stake president, who was a friend of hers, performed the baptism). The Lord knew that each of these individuals were ready to make covenants with him, inspired his servants, and prepared a miracle. My point in sharing these stories is to invite you to elevate your vision and expect a miracle as we engage in the work that our Heavenly Father has invited us to participate in. 
President Spencer W Kimball -  “The spirit of our work must be urgency!”

When the Lord said, “Lengthen your stride, quicken your pace, heighten your reach, widen your vision, and stretch your capacity,” he was in reality saying “expect a miracle,” for these are the stuff from which miracles are made.
The prophet says, “DO IT,” and he indicates the time is NOW. And expect the miracle.”
Brother and Sisters, I leave you with this invitation and my testimony. I invite you to gather Israel THIS WEEK, and expect the miracle
