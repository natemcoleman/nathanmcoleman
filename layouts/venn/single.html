How To Play

Make every cluster a single color. Drag a circle to turn it. Every point on it moves together, and it snaps to the nearest quarter turn when you let go. Finished clusters glow.

  • A new puzzle every day at midnight. Monday is easiest, Sunday is hardest.
  • The goal at the top is the fewest moves possible. Stuck? Tap Hint to see which circle to turn (+1 move), and again to see how far (+1 more). Reset goes back to the start.
  • After you solve, you can watch the shortest possible solution and see how everyone else did (anonymously; see Settings → Privacy).

Privacy

Venn has no accounts. Your progress and stats are saved only in this browser.

When you solve the daily puzzle, your move count, hints and turns are sent with a random ID so everyone can compare results. No name or email is attached. To stop spam, your IP address is kept for up to an hour, then deleted.

Feedback is stored with the email address only if you give one, and it's only used to reply. This site uses Google Analytics to count visits.
