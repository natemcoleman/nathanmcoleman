How To Play

Make every cluster a single color. Drag a circle to turn it. Every point on it moves together, and they snap into place when you let go.

  • A new puzzle every day at midnight. Monday is easiest, Sunday is hardest.
  • Hints are unlimited and show the best next turn, but each one adds 2 to your moves.
  • After you solve, you can watch the shortest possible solution.
