On July 20, 1969, Eagle’s computer was steering toward the boulder-covered flank of West Crater. Neil Armstrong took over, flew across it at around 100 m, and kept going west hunting for smooth ground — touching down about 550 m beyond it. Capcom Charlie Duke called out “60 seconds” and then “30 seconds” of fuel; Eagle landed with roughly 20–25 seconds to spare.

The real West Crater is about 190 m across — it’s scaled up here so you can see it.
